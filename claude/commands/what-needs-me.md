What waits on me: my open to-dos, then what may soon need me and is due again, as the briefing lists them, each looked up afresh in the copy; then what is new that involves me, from what_may_need_you and the conversations behind it. Leave out of what is new everything a saved report already showed, told or used, as the `report` skill counts it: a point given me before is not news again. Answer in the first three parts of a report, headed as the `report` skill heads them, each left out where it holds nothing, speaking to me as "you":

1. **Your to-dos**, oldest first, each with how long it has waited.
2. **What may soon need you**: the most pressing and newest first.
3. **What is new**: what involves me, and nothing a report already gave me.
