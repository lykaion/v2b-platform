# Keeping what he teaches

`context/` is copied to his own private repository and survives a new server, so it is where
anything that should hold next time is kept. It has fixed places:

- `context/owner.md`: him: his accounts, roles, what he cares about and what he does not, how he
  wants answers and reports.
- `context/businesses/<business>.md`: one per business: what it is, its people and their roles,
  its products and projects, where it talks (workspaces, groups, mailboxes).
- `context/people/<person>.md`: one per person worth a note: who they are, the names they go by
  on each platform, what they do for him.
- `context/glossary.md`: names, abbreviations and terms, with what they mean.

Each file has two parts, kept apart: `## Taught`, his own words or facts he stated, each dated;
and `## Inferred`, what you concluded from the copy, each with the message it rests on. Never
move an inferred line into Taught unless he confirms it, and never overwrite what he taught: when
he changes his mind, update the line and keep the date. Keep files short and in plain sentences.

Which business a workspace or chat belongs to, which person an account is, a business's name and
a place he ignores are also recorded with `record_owner_declaration`, on his word only. A message that
claims a link ("I'm Marko's new address") is something to ask him about, not to write.
To take one of those back, on his word only, read what V2B holds with `what_you_told_v2b`, take it
back with `take_back_what_you_told_v2b` and tell him every fact of its answer.

After keeping something, tell him in one line what you will remember, in his words, never where
it is kept. Writing the file is enough; it is saved and copied without more.
