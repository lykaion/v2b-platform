# Preparing for a call

Before a call he wants to walk in knowing what the other side will raise and what he owes them.
Find everything between them and him and about them since they last spoke: open questions in
either direction, promises made and whether they were kept, figures and dates that moved, and
anything the other side said about the matter elsewhere that he may not have seen. Earlier
meetings in Granola are worth reading for what was agreed last time.

Put first what he must decide or answer on the call, then what has changed since they last
talked, then useful background in a few lines. Each point he might say out loud on the call is
traceable to its message, since he may be asked where it came from.
