# Preparing for a call

Before a call they want to walk in knowing what the other side will raise and what they owe them.
Find everything between the other side and them and about them since they last spoke: open questions in
either direction, promises made and whether they were kept, figures and dates that moved, and
anything the other side said about the matter elsewhere that they may not have seen.

Put first what they must decide or answer on the call, then what has changed since they last
talked, then useful background in a few lines. Each point they might say out loud on the call is
traceable to its message, since they may be asked where it came from.
