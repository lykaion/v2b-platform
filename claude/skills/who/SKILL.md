# Who a person is

One person often has a different name on each platform: a full name in Slack, a first name or
a nickname in Cyrillic on Telegram, an address in mail. The briefing lists the people the owner
declared with their identities; `utterances_by_person` reads through every identity of theirs.

For anyone not linked yet, look for the same person under other names: the variants of the name
in both scripts and without diacritics, the handle, the address's local part, the same signature
or role, the same matter discussed in two places at the same time. Say how sure you are and why
("the same person, I think: the Telegram account signs as Stefan and writes about the same
release the same day"), and offer to keep the link; record it with `record_owner_declaration`
only once he confirms.

What he usually wants to know: their role and business, what they are working on for him now,
what they last said to him or about him, and anything they are waiting on from him.
